Featured Project

Onyx Auto: End-to-End Data & Analytics Engineering Platform

Business problem: dealership reporting data is fragmented across sales, inventory, parts, sellers, customers, and operational source tables.

Solution: a source-to-dashboard analytics platform that captures operational data, ingests it into Snowflake, transforms it with dbt, orchestrates refreshes with Airflow, and serves Power BI dashboards.

  • Operational PHP/MySQL source application and relational database.
  • Automated ELT pipeline using Airbyte, Snowflake, dbt, Airflow, and Docker.
  • Executive dashboard layer for sales, inventory, parts, and seller-risk reporting.
