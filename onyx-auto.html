Onyx Auto: End-to-End Data & Analytics Engineering Platform

Onyx Auto is an end-to-end analytics engineering project that simulates a used car dealership data platform. The system captures operational dealership data in a PHP and MySQL application, ingests it into Snowflake with Airbyte, transforms it through dbt staging, intermediate, and mart layers, orchestrates refreshes with Airflow, and delivers executive reporting in Power BI.

Business Problem

Turning dealership operations into reliable reporting.

Used car dealerships need visibility into inventory, sales, parts activity, seller quality, and operational performance. Raw transactional systems are useful for recording day-to-day activity, but they are not always designed for analytical reporting.

Sales visibilityHow much income and gross sales income has the dealership generated?
Inventory healthHow long are vehicles remaining unsold?
Sales performanceWhich sales agents are driving monthly performance?
Seller riskWhich sellers are associated with higher parts costs?

Power BI Reporting Layer

Business-facing dashboards built from curated marts.

Power BI is the final consumption layer for the platform. The report connects to curated Snowflake mart models built by dbt and presents dealership performance through executive, sales, inventory, parts, and seller-risk report pages.

Why this matters: Business users consume curated metrics instead of querying raw operational tables.

Architecture Overview

Source-to-dashboard analytics workflow.

The architecture begins with operational dealership data captured in a PHP frontend and stored in MySQL. Airbyte syncs MySQL source tables into Snowflake, where raw data lands in a dedicated raw schema, dbt transforms raw data into staging, intermediate, and mart models, Power BI connects to the marts, and Airflow coordinates refreshes with Teams-based failure alerts.

Source Application and Database Design

Operational system modeled before analytics begins.

The source system is a PHP dealership application backed by a MySQL transactional database. It captures customers, vehicles, vendors, parts orders, users, employee roles, and sales transactions.

Airbyte Ingestion Layer

Incremental MySQL-to-Snowflake synchronization.

Airbyte moves operational data from MySQL into Snowflake. The connection syncs dealership streams such as customers, vehicles, sales, parts orders, vendors, manufacturers, users, and employee role tables.

Why this matters: Operational records can be incrementally synchronized into the warehouse without manual exports.

dbt Transformation Layer

Layered modeling from raw tables to reporting marts.

The dbt project transforms Airbyte-loaded MySQL data in Snowflake into analytics-ready dealership models. The project uses staging, intermediate, and mart layers to build clean references, reusable business logic, dimensions, facts, aggregates, and reporting-ready outputs.

Why this matters: Raw source tables are converted into tested, documented, reusable reporting models.

Staging

Standardizes raw source tables and prepares clean references for downstream logic.

Intermediate

Applies reusable business logic for customers, employees, vehicle status, financials, parts costs, and sales enrichment.

Marts

Creates reporting-ready dimensions, facts, and aggregates for Power BI dashboards.

Testing & documentation

Documents models and validates uniqueness, non-null fields, and relationships.

Airflow Orchestration and Monitoring

Scheduled workflow from ingestion to semantic model refresh.

Apache Airflow orchestrates the daily Onyx Auto pipeline. The DAG runs Airbyte sync, waits for ingestion to complete, runs the dbt build, refreshes the Power BI semantic model, and sends Microsoft Teams alerts if a pipeline task fails.

Why this matters: The refresh process is scheduled, observable, and failure-aware.
Security note: Before publishing, review alert screenshots for sensitive identifiers, tokens, webhook URLs, and internal connection details. Redact when needed.

Key Outcomes

What this project demonstrates.

Onyx Auto demonstrates the full analytics engineering lifecycle: source-system design, ingestion, cloud warehousing, layered dbt transformation, dimensional modeling, dashboard development, orchestration, and monitoring.

  • Data reliability: Modeled raw operational data into stable reporting-ready Snowflake marts.
  • Automation: Orchestrated ingestion, dbt builds, and Power BI refreshes through Airflow.
  • Reporting usability: Delivered executive and operational Power BI reports for sales, inventory, parts, and seller-risk analysis.
  • Observability: Added Teams-based failure notifications for pipeline monitoring.
  • Stakeholder decision support: Translated operational dealership events into business-facing KPIs and reporting pages.

Recruiter takeaway

The work maps directly to analytics engineering responsibilities and supports data engineering expectations around ingestion, orchestration, warehouse design, and pipeline reliability.
