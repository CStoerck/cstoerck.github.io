Onyx Auto: End-to-End Dealership Analytics Engineering Platform

Onyx Auto is an end-to-end analytics engineering platform that simulates how a used car dealership can convert raw operational transactions into automated business reporting. The project captures dealership activity through a PHP and MySQL source application, ingests the operational data into Snowflake with Airbyte, transforms it into analytics-ready models using dbt, visualizes dealership performance in Power BI, and orchestrates the daily refresh workflow with Apache Airflow.

Executive Summary

From dealership transactions to trusted reporting.

Onyx Auto demonstrates the full journey from business activity to decision-ready analytics. A dealership records sales, vehicles, customers, vendors, and parts orders in an operational system. That data is then moved, cleaned, modeled, automated, and delivered through dashboards that business users can use to monitor sales performance, inventory health, parts costs, and seller risk.

Business Problem

Operational data is useful, but it is not automatically analytical.

Used car dealerships generate operational data across vehicle inventory, customer records, sales transactions, vendors, parts orders, and employee activity. While this data supports day-to-day operations, transactional tables are not always designed for executive reporting or cross-functional analysis.

Sales visibility
How much net income and gross sales income has the dealership generated?
Inventory health
Which vehicles are staying in inventory too long?
Sales performance
Which sales agents are driving monthly performance?
Seller risk
Which sellers are associated with higher downstream parts costs?

Solution Overview

A source-to-dashboard analytics platform.

The platform follows a complete analytics engineering workflow. Dealership users enter operational records through a PHP frontend application. Those records are stored in a MySQL transactional database. Airbyte replicates the source data into Snowflake, dbt transforms the raw tables into clean reporting models, Power BI connects to the final mart layer, and Apache Airflow coordinates the pipeline so each refresh step runs in the correct order.

Architecture Summary

Clear separation across source, ingestion, warehouse, transformation, reporting, and monitoring.

The architecture keeps each layer responsible for one job. The source application captures dealership activity, Airbyte moves source data into the warehouse, Snowflake stores raw and transformed data separately, dbt builds reporting-ready models, Power BI communicates the final metrics, and Airflow automates the refresh sequence with Microsoft Teams failure alerts.

Analytics Engineering Workflow

Layered modeling from raw tables to reporting marts.

Airbyte handles MySQL-to-Snowflake ingestion using an incremental sync pattern based on updated source records. Snowflake serves as the analytical warehouse, with raw ingested data separated from transformed analytics models so the pipeline preserves source fidelity while producing business-ready datasets.

dbt, which stands for data build tool, organizes the transformation process into staging, intermediate, and mart layers. Staging models standardize raw Airbyte-loaded tables, intermediate models apply reusable business logic, and mart models produce the final facts, dimensions, and aggregates consumed by Power BI.

Staging
Standardizes raw source tables and prepares clean references for downstream logic.
Intermediate
Applies reusable business logic for customers, employee roles, vehicle status, financials, parts costs, and enriched sales.
Marts
Creates reporting-ready dimensions, facts, and aggregate models for dashboard consumption.
Testing & documentation
Documents models and validates important uniqueness, non-null, and relationship expectations.

Dashboard and Reporting Layer

Business-facing Power BI reports built from curated marts.

The Power BI report connects to curated Snowflake mart models and organizes dealership performance into executive, monthly sales, active inventory, part statistics, and seller history pages. The dashboard tracks metrics such as net income, gross sales income, vehicles sold, average profit per vehicle, average days in inventory, parts spend, inventory value, and seller-level risk indicators.

Orchestration and Automation

Scheduled refresh workflow with monitoring signals.

Apache Airflow orchestrates the daily pipeline by triggering the Airbyte sync, waiting for ingestion to complete, running the dbt build, refreshing the Power BI semantic model, and sending Microsoft Teams alerts if a task fails. This makes the project more production-like because the workflow is not a set of disconnected manual steps; it runs as a defined sequence with dependencies, scheduling, monitoring, and alerting.
